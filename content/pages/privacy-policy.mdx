      Grow and Glow Retreats ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is

      We may collect the following types of information:


      - **Email address** — when you subscribe to our mailing list or submit a

      - **Usage data** — such as pages visited, time spent on the site, and

      ## How We Use Your Information


      We use the information we collect to:


      - Send you updates about upcoming retreats, events, and offerings.

      When you provide your email address, you consent to receiving periodic

      We may use third-party services (such as email marketing platforms and
      website analytics tools) to help operate our business. These services may
      have access to your personal information only to perform tasks on our
      behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for other purposes.


      ## Cookies


      Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your
      browsing experience and analyze site traffic. You can control cookie
      preferences through your browser settings.


      ## Data Security


      We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from
      unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of
      transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee
      absolute security.


      ## Your Rights


      You have the right to:


      - Request access to the personal information we hold about you.

      - Request correction or deletion of your personal information.

      - Unsubscribe from our email communications at any time.

      - Request that we stop processing your personal information.

      Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of
      18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
